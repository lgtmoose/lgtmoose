# Code of conduct

LGTMoose follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and give critique of the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository, or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
